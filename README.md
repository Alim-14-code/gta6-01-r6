A dusk-lit open-city crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the
[make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

**Port Alegre** — three islands joined by bridges, a harbor full of boats, a
lighthouse on Sunset Isle, and a crew that owes the harbor boss. Play as
**Kai Marlin**: drive and walk a living city, steal cars, dodge the police,
and finish all six story jobs — delivery, dockside chase, lighthouse run,
quiet tail, getaway, and the harbor heist.

- Open world on foot and by car: traffic, pedestrians, boats, bridges
- Wanted system 0–5 stars: patrol, chase, roadblocks, helicopter
- 6 story missions with timers, objectives, success and fail states
- HUD with minimap, cash, health, speed and mission tracker
- WebAudio synth sound: engine, sirens, mission stingers — no audio files
- Save to the browser (localStorage); pause, busted/wasted, story complete

Play it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
Controls: WASD/arrows to drive & walk, E to enter a car / start a job,
P to pause, H to honk, M to mute.

MIT licensed. All city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sounds
are original.
